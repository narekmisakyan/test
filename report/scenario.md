# Project scenario: Data Knowledge (as shown in scenario6102751.pdf during the exam)

## Data Knowledge

Data Knowledge is a company with the mission of leveraging the power of big data through advanced data analytics solutions. By analysing the wants, needs, desires, and frustrations of social media users, Data Knowledge can transform raw and unstructured data into reliable predictions that enable organizations to utilize consumer behaviours and insights.

However, they have not been as effective in using data to their own advantage, now that the company has over 300 staff. In fact, the head of the data science division raised concerns in the last senior leadership team meeting. The concerns were regarding the lack of visibility of who is working on what projects, poor capacity planning, and claims of an overworked team with low morale due to the constant juggling of commitments and firefighting issues.

As a way of addressing this, the data science division has been authorized to start a project to develop a time-recording solution and a set of reports on project and task assignments along with resource utilization and capacity planning. The reports are expected to offer interactive and real-time insights that can enable data-driven decisions around the utilization and planning of resources.

Data Knowledge has a defined framework for the management of projects. This is expected to be a simple business change project, taking no more than three months in the current financial year to complete.

Since Data Knowledge has in-house data analysis and app development capabilities, as well as clear requirements for this project, the project will be led by the head of data science. They will work with the development division in an iterative and incremental approach. This is to ensure that the solution can be deployed quickly to several new projects and enhanced during its roll-out to the rest of the business.

The high-level project plan is as follows:

- Stage 1 (initiation): product backlog; Minimum Viable Product (MVP) definition
- Stage 2:
  - timebox 1: prototype of time-recording application; mock-up of resource management reports
  - timebox 2: first release of time-recording application and reports (must-have features); pilot
  - timebox 3: enhancements following pilot
  - timebox 4: demonstration; roll-out
- Stage 3: product backlog (following roll-out); closure.

The main output from the project is to be a time-recording application and set of resource management reports available to all business units.

## Additional Information

- The **Chief Executive Officer** leads the Senior Leadership Team and, together with her team, makes decisions about which projects Data Knowledge should invest in over the next financial year.
- The **Head of Finance** wants to ensure the success of the Resource Management Project as he is concerned that projects are being delayed due to under-resourcing. In addition, he will be the final approver of the project finances, after the Senior Leadership Team has given its initial approval.
- The **Accountant** who reviews the outline business case of every project, before it is submitted to the Senior Leadership Team for approval. They are responsible for the ongoing review of project finances, including spend against budget and that project plans are realistic and achievable within the project budget.
- The **Head of Data Science** understands the business need for the Resource Management Project and has a keen interest in ensuring that the right data is collected about each project. She ensures that the data is used effectively by Data Knowledge.
- The **Head of the Development Division** has a team that will be developing the time recording app. Their team will generate the required management reports using AccurateReports Ltd's off-the-shelf reporting product. All project teams across Data Knowledge will record their time using this app.
- The **Head of Human Resources (HR)** will be using the data provided by the resource management reports to predict future resource needs. His role is to ensure that Data Knowledge has sufficient resource capacity and capability to meet its needs.
- The **Head of Project Management** is a new appointment. She is starting to work with the Project Management Community to understand their issues and how they can be better supported. She is hoping that the data from the resource management reports will provide new insights into the project management challenges being faced, and any new skills required to meet future business needs.
- **Project Management Community** is made up of a large number of project managers and other project team members. The community meets regularly to discuss lessons learned and how better to apply project management best practice to the delivery of iterative/incremental projects. Some of these project managers deliver projects alongside their business as usual activities. Several members of this community will be part of the pilot for the time recording app.
- The **Development Manager** has experience of delivering app projects for Data Knowledge's clients. They are certified in a variety of agile methods and have experience of combining project management with iterative and incremental delivery to meet customer needs. They are a member of the Project Management Community.
- The **Agile coach** is experienced in iterative and incremental delivery and acts as a coach to all teams across the Development Division. She will advise the Resource Management Project on how best to deliver the minimum viable product and prototype the time recording app.
- The **Data Science Advisor** has been working for Data Knowledge since it was a startup company. She advises Data Knowledge's extensive client base on how best to utilize data to predict future customer behaviours, using this knowledge to gain competitive advantage.
- The **Data Administrator** works alongside the app development teams and the customer's representative, tracking the delivery and testing of requirements. She reports progress against the product backlog to the project manager.
- The **HR Manager** has worked on a number of small HR-led projects. His personal development plan includes the need to extend his experience in working on projects so that he can take on more responsibility at some point in the future.
- The **IT Manager** is responsible for managing the IT infrastructure and networks for the staff of Data Knowledge. He works closely with Purple Services Ltd (the cloud services provider) to ensure that service level agreements are met to support Data Knowledge's applications.
- The **CEO of AccurateReports Ltd** sold their off-the-shelf management reporting system to Data Knowledge a few years ago. This system interfaces with Data Knowledge's apps and produces reports for Data Knowledge's clients. A team in the Development Division maintains the licensing and ensures appropriate upgrades to software.
- The **Purple Services Ltd's Account Manager**: Data Knowledge purchase cloud services from Purple Services. The Account Manager will need to be consulted with regard to any changes to apps that could affect these services.
